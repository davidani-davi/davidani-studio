# Saved face protection

Open **Models & pose references**, choose a model and pose, and click **Face protection** beneath a front, side, or full reference photo.

- **Garment boundary** (amber line): place above all clothing.
- **Hair blend**: adjusts the band ending at the amber line. Keep the entire face above its green edge; widening the band softens hair joins.
- Review the image, check the confirmation, and **Save photo**.

Each view stores its own settings in the shared reference library. Future Simple garment swaps reuse them. Reopening and saving preserves exact image-row values, even when percentages display rounded. Replacing a photo requires a fresh review; disabling protection removes its saved boundary.

The same review controls appear when saving generated reference sets. Back views do not need a face boundary. Inspect generated results as well: protected-pixel verification alone does not establish visual quality.

DET70088's reviewed Celine front reference uses a 25% boundary (748 rows) and a 48-row hair blend on its 2000 × 2992 original. Keep these settings unless deliberately reviewing a new boundary.
